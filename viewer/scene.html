Waiting Room — live scene

Waiting Room runs in a small window that floats above your terminal.

Chrome and Edge only. This window can be minimised afterwards.

Movement carries meaning: leaves and water move while a turn runs, everything settles when idle, birds lift when a turn finishes (and sometimes on their own), and the window reset is a sunrise. Clouds always drift, occasional rings spread across the lake, and fireflies glow near the reeds at dusk.
